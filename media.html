Short biography

Collin M. Constantine is a College Assistant Professor of Economics at Girton College, University of Cambridge. His research lies at the intersection of international macroeconomics, finance, and political economy, with a focus on how foreign exchange constraint, fiscal dominance, bank behaviour, and inequality shape economic outcomes in developing economies. He is Co-Editor of Economics, serves on the editorial board of the Journal of Stratification Economics, and is a member of the Central Bank Research Association (CEBRA).
