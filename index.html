Collin M. Constantine is a College Assistant Professor of Economics at Girton College, University of Cambridge. His research lies at the intersection of international macroeconomics, finance, and the political economy of development.

What I Do

My research helps shape a macroeconomics of and for developing economies, where standard assumptions often travel poorly. It treats foreign exchange scarcity, import dependence, fiscal dominance, and financial dollarisation as central features of their macro-dynamics.

This research explains why standard measures of overheating, fiscal space, and policy effectiveness often prove unreliable in developing economies.
